Executive verdict

Hackathon decision
Submit the implemented prototype.

It has substantive AI, verified X Layer Testnet contracts, a working dynamic-fee path, a public site, and unusually honest measurements. Present it as a filing-aware risk-control prototype—not as the completed rumor-safe LP Risk Autopilot described in the final design.

Product decision
Pivot / go validate.

Do not expand source coverage yet. First prove that event awareness beats a volatility-only controller and that one external LP or pool operator cares enough to test it.

Most likely judge objection: the project protects a builder-owned mock testnet pool from a risk whose median measured effect is economically tiny relative to pool TVL; the final differentiators that answer that objection remain designed rather than implemented.

Crypto necessity gut-check

What breaks if blockchain is removed?

The filing parser, news graph, warnings, and backtest can all run off-chain. What breaks is synchronous enforcement inside a permissionless pool, a common policy record that independent consumers can verify, and composable settlement of the resulting action.

Conclusion: blockchain is necessary for the bounded execution and shared-risk-record product. It is ornamental if Tinjau remains only an alerts dashboard.

Persist, cut, and prove

PersistKeep the EDGAR agreement pipeline, existing registry/hook, deterministic fee bounds and decay, X Layer deployment, and explicit observed-versus-counterfactual labeling.
Cut nowDo not attempt broad news/social coverage, x402, Agentic Wallet, Exchange OS, a general SDK, or partnership-style claims before the deadline.
Prove nextImplement one deterministic NORMAL / WATCH / PROTECT replay with a rumor safety case and a static-vs-volatility-vs-event-aware comparison on a frozen dataset.
Validate nextInterview five to ten X Layer or xStocks LPs, market makers, and pool operators about their current event-risk workflow. Ask for a replay dataset, integration test, or signed pilot—not an expression of interest.
Integrate firstReuse SEC EDGAR, OKX reference/index endpoints, direct X Layer reads, and Uniswap v4. Build only Tinjau's evidence-state policy, risk-record interface, and reproducible benchmark.

Missing evidence ranked by decision impact

  1. Incremental protection: Does the event-aware policy beat static and volatility-only policies after false-positive cost?
  2. External demand: Will an LP/operator provide data, run a pilot, integrate the record, or commit liquidity?
  3. Real venue relevance: Can the policy operate against executable production routes and meaningful depth rather than mock assets?
  4. Safety quality: What are the unsupported-PROTECT, missed-event, contradiction, stale-data, and source-independence error rates?
  5. Distribution: Is the reusable record actually consumed by another X Layer application?
