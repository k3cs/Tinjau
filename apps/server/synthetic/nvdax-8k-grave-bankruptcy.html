Item 1.03. Bankruptcy or Receivership.

On August 17, 2026, NVIDIA Corporation (the "Company") and certain of its direct and indirect subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 of Title 11 of the United States Code in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware (the "Bankruptcy Court"). The Company has requested that the Chapter 11 cases be jointly administered.

The Company intends to continue to operate its business as "debtor-in-possession" under the jurisdiction of the Bankruptcy Court and in accordance with the applicable provisions of the Bankruptcy Code and orders of the Bankruptcy Court.

The filing of the Chapter 11 petitions constituted an event of default that accelerated the Company's obligations under its outstanding senior notes, in an aggregate principal amount of $8,250,000,000.

Item 2.04. Triggering Events That Accelerate or Increase a Direct Financial Obligation.

The information set forth in Item 1.03 above is incorporated by reference into this Item 2.04.

Item 3.01. Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule.

On August 17, 2026, the Company received written notice from The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC stating that, as a result of the Chapter 11 filing described in Item 1.03, Nasdaq has determined to commence proceedings to delist the Company's common stock. Trading in the Company's common stock is expected to be suspended at the opening of business on August 24, 2026.
